## 1. NASA-ISRO SAR (NISAR) Data Products & Specifications

Products and specifications provided by NASA/JPL and the Alaska Satellite
Facility (ASF) are governed by the **NASA Open Data Policy (Public Domain /
US Government Work)**.

- Mission home: <https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/>
- ASF documentation: <https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/>
- Product specification: NASA SDS Document D-102272, *Level-2 Geocoded
  UNWRapped Interferogram (GUNW)*.

Earthquake event catalog data retrieved from the U.S. Geological Survey
(USGS) Comprehensive Earthquake Catalog (ComCat) is in the **Public Domain**
under United States Government open data policies.

- Source: <https://earthquake.usgs.gov/>
