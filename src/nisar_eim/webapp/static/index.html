What this radar reveals — and what it cannot claim

Radar interferometry measures phase difference, not physical displacement directly. Understanding each layer is essential to scientific integrity.

Phase (rad)

Unwrapped interferometric phase

The difference in radar echo phase between two passes, in radians. It responds to changes in line-of-sight distance, but also wraps ionospheric delays, tropospheric water vapor, orbit geometry, and unwrapping offsets.

⚠️ Not a displacement map in millimetres. Not proven ground motion.

Coherence (0–1)

Interferometric coherence magnitude

A dimensionless 0–1 metric of electromagnetic similarity between passes. High coherence indicates stable ground reflectors; water, dense vegetation, and physical surface disruption decorrelate the signal.

⚠️ A reliability indicator, not a measurement of deformation.

Quality mask

Validity and subswath bitmasks

The Level-2 GUNW mask decodes open water surfaces, reference and secondary subswaths (1–4), and RSLC processing anomalies. It isolates unmeasurable regions from true radar returns.

⚠️ “Valid” means mathematically measurable—not scientifically noise-free.

Components

Connected phase domains

Connected components partition the scene into spatially continuous regions where the 2π phase ambiguity was solved. Separate components can carry unknown integer cycle offsets.

⚠️ Disconnected islands cannot be assumed to share the same phase baseline.

Strict Scientific Guardrail: In this application, phase-to-displacement conversion is intentionally unvalidated. Without external GPS networks, tropospheric delay models (GACOS), and verified ground control, raw InSAR phase must never be presented as validated ground motion.
