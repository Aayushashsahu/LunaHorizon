## Application Usage & Scientific Integrity Note
This visual asset serves exclusively as an illustrative planetary hero background for the landing dashboard. It demonstrates the macroscopic Earth context of the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR) mission.

**Scientific Guardrail:**
The hero globe is strictly illustrative context. It is **not** a live telemetry feed, real-time satellite position, or a representation of radar phase. The verified, georeferenced NISAR Level-2 GUNW interferograms and measurements are loaded, rendered, and analyzed exclusively inside the **Observation Explorer** (`#view-explorer`).
